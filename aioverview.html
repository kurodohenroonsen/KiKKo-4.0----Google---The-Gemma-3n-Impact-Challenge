    Apis mellifera, commonly known as the Western honey bee, is a social insect species native to Europe, Africa, and the Middle East. It's the most widely domesticated species of honey bee and is known for producing honey and pollinating plants. 
    Origin: The exact origin of Apis mellifera is debated, but it's generally believed to have originated in Africa or Asia and then spread to Europe and the Middle East. 
    Weight: Individual honey bees weigh between 72-100 milligrams. 
    Life Expectancy: Queen bees can live for several years (1-5 years), while worker bees typically live for a few weeks to a few months, with longer lifespans in the winter. 
    Size: Worker bees are typically 12-15 mm in length. Drones are slightly larger, and queens are the largest, reaching 18-20 mm. 
    Flight Speed: Honey bees can fly at an average speed of 15-20 miles per hour (24-32 kilometers per hour). 
    Fun Facts: 
    1. To make one pound of honey, bees must visit about 2 million flowers, according to Swansons Nursery.
    2. Honey bees communicate the location of food sources to each other through a "waggle dance".
    3. Honey bees can see ultraviolet light, which helps them find pollen and nectar.

    What is the origin of Apis mellifera?
    A. mellifera, the most common domesticated species, was first domesticated before 2600 BC and was the third insect to have its genome mapped. It seems to have originated in eastern tropical Africa and spread from there to Europe and eastwards into Asia to the Tian Shan range.

    What is Apis mellifera Linnaeus?
    honey-bearing
    The western honey bee or European honey bee (Apis mellifera) is the most common of the 7–12 species of honey bees worldwide. The genus name Apis is Latin for 'bee', and mellifera is the Latin for 'honey-bearing' or 'honey-carrying', referring to the species' production of honey.

    What are the characteristics of Apis mellifera?
    Generally, Apis mellifera are red/brown with black bands and orange yellow rings on abdomen. They have hair on thorax and less hair on abdomen. They also have a pollen basket on their hind legs. Honeybee legs are mostly dark brown/black.
